1. WHAT THE PROJECT IS
----------------------
FormFlow lets you sketch 3D scenes in the air with your hands, using only a
webcam. It also builds an approximate 3D model from outlines drawn in Front,
Side and Top views (a "visual hull").

  Mode 1 - 3D Sketch       Draw curves and shapes directly in 3D with gestures.
  Mode 2 - 3-Plane Hull    Draw closed outlines in two or three views (or add a
                           photo of the object); the 3D shape builds itself.

No model is trained. Only the hand landmark detector (MediaPipe) is pre-built.
Everything else (gestures, smoothing, reconstruction, meshing) is our own code.

Project folders:
  code/            the application (web app + Python server + tests)
  code/README.md   full technical README
  Presentation/    final.html (the slides), ppt.html (idea slides)
  docs/            idea brief, PRD, architecture, pipeline
  code/exports/    saved OBJ / STL files appear here

4. FIRST SCREEN: PRACTICE MODE
------------------------------
The app first asks you to prove tracking works:
  - Show your hand to the camera.
  - Pinch (thumb + index finger touching) and hold ~0.5 second.
  - Make a fist and hold ~0.5 second.
While you do this it measures your open and pinched finger spacing and sets
your PERSONAL pinch thresholds automatically. Then the workspace opens.

Useful: the guide / help button in the app shows the full controls table.
The Settings panel lets you choose dominant hand, distance (near / medium /
far / auto) and sensitivity. Reach calibration (hold the hand at two opposite
corners of your working area) is for far-away cameras or projectors.


5. HAND GESTURES (what to do with your hands)
---------------------------------------------
  Drawing hand (default: right, cyan marker)
    Pinch  thumb + index ........... draw a curve / place a shape / press a button
    Grab   thumb + middle finger ... pick up and move an object
  Navigation hand (left, magenta marker)
    Fist + move .................... orbit (rotate) the camera
    Peace sign + move .............. pan the camera
  Both hands
    Pinch with both, move apart / together ... zoom

Placing a shape: choose the tool, pinch at the centre, drag outward, release.
Mouse fallback (no camera needed to test the UI):
    left button = pinch, Shift+left = grab, right = fist, middle = peace.

7. DEMO SCRIPT (about 5 minutes) - what to do and say
-----------------------------------------------------
  Step 1  Start and practice mode (30 s)
    Do: run.bat, allow the camera, pinch and fist.
    Say: "Hand landmarks come from MediaPipe. We turn them into gestures with
         palm-normalised ratios and hysteresis, so it works at any distance."

  Step 2  Free 3D drawing (60 s)
    Do: pick Freehand, pinch and draw a curve in the air. Release.
    Say: "The pointer is smoothed with a One-Euro filter, and a grasp latch
         makes the stroke start where I aimed, not where the fingers drifted."

  Step 3  Shapes and editing (45 s)
    Do: press 5 (cube): pinch, drag out, release. Then 6 (sphere).
        Use Grab (thumb + middle) to move one. Press Z to undo.
    Say: "Solids are convex meshes with outward winding, ready to export."

  Step 4  Camera control (30 s)
    Do: make a fist with the other hand and move to orbit; peace sign to pan;
        pinch with both hands and spread them to zoom.
    Say: "Navigation is on the second hand so drawing and viewing never clash."

  Step 5  Join lines (30 s)
    Do: draw a loop that almost closes. A prompt appears: pinch Join (or press J).
    Say: "Hands never land exactly on the start; the app finds near ends and
         closes the loop. The hull needs closed outlines."

  Step 6  Multi-view reconstruction - the main feature (90 s)
    Do: switch to the 3-Plane Hull mode. Draw a closed outline in the FRONT cell
        (for example a rectangle), then another in the SIDE cell. The 3D
        PREVIEW cell builds the shape by itself. Add a TOP outline for a
        tighter result. Or press "Photos -> 3D" and pick up to three photos
        (front, side, top) of one object: the outlines are found and the 3D
        model is built in one step. For a bottle or cup, one front photo with
        "One view: Round" spins it into a round solid.
    Say: "Each outline is a silhouette. We rasterise them, intersect the three
         extruded volumes in a 64-cube voxel grid (visual hull), keep the
         largest piece, and make a smooth mesh with surface nets. If views
         disagree by more than the tolerance we warn instead of guessing."
    Show: the warning/score text (per-view IoU) and the triangle count.

  Step 7  Export and view the file (45 s)
    Do: press E (or use the Export STL button). The toast confirms the saved files.
        Open  code\exports  in File Explorer: formflow_<time>.obj and .stl.
        Open the STL in Windows 3D Viewer, Blender, MeshLab or a slicer.
    Say: "Exports go over a WebSocket to the Python backend, which writes the
         exact bytes. STL is binary, OBJ is text."

10. HOW IT WORKS - ONE PARAGRAPH PER STAGE (for questions)
----------------------------------------------------------
  1 Capture     Webcam frame -> optional zoom-and-follow crop around the hands
                (square ROI, 9 palm sizes) so small distant hands are detected.
  2 Detect      MediaPipe Hand Landmarker gives 21 landmarks per hand (image
                and metric 3D). Optional Pose Landmarker gives wrist/elbow.
  3 Track       Remove duplicate detections, keep identity by position, vote on
                left/right, require 3 frames to accept a hand, hold 0.25 s if
                lost, ignore smaller bystander hands.
  4 Gesture     palm = |wrist - middle knuckle|; rI = |thumb - index| / palm;
                rM = |thumb - middle| / palm. Pinch when rI < 0.30 (release at
                0.45), grab uses rM, fist = all fingertips closer to wrist than
                their middle joints, peace = index+middle extended.
  5 Cursor      One-Euro filter (cutoff rises with speed), cursor anchored to
                the palm, glitch rejection (jump > 0.18), grasp latch.
  6 Map         Reach region -> full screen with equal speed on both axes.
  7 Sketch      Orbit camera (FOV 40.6 deg), ray unprojection onto a drawing
                plane, segment picking, undo log, convex solid primitives.
  8 Hull        Classify outline plane -> align shared dimensions -> even-odd
                scanline raster -> voxel AND of the 3 masks -> largest
                connected component -> IoU re-projection score.
  9 Mesh        Pad grid, [1 4 1]/6 blur on each axis, iso 0.5, one vertex per
                mixed cell, quads on sign-changing edges -> triangles.
 10 Export      ASCII OBJ (5 decimals) and binary STL via the Python backend.

  Limits: a visual hull cannot recover concave parts or hidden features; the
  result is an approximate prototype for ideation, not manufacturing CAD.


11. TROUBLESHOOTING
-------------------
  Black / no camera       Use Chrome or Edge, open http://127.0.0.1:8000 (not a
                          file), click Allow, close other camera apps.
  Hand not detected       Keep Settings > Light correction on Auto (it brightens
                          a hand that is dark against a window or lamp). Laptop
                          webcam: Distance = Near. Small hands: Sensitivity high.
                          Press D to see the light-correction level and dropouts.
  Pinch triggers by itself / not at all
                          Repeat practice mode so personal thresholds are
                          re-measured; keep the hand facing the camera.
  Cursor jumpy            Increase smoothing in Settings.
  "Port already in use"   run.bat --port 9000
  Export does not save    Check the server window is still running. The link
                          indicator in the HUD shows if the backend is online.
  Python not found        Install Python 3.12 and re-run run.bat.
  Slow / low frame rate   Close other tabs, plug in the laptop, use Chrome with
                          hardware acceleration on (GPU delegate is preferred).
